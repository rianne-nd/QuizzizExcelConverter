Question: Which command adds execute permission for owner and read permission for group and others.
Option: chmod u=x,go=r file.txt
Option: chmod u+x,go+r file.txt*
Option: chmod u-x,go-r file.txt
Option: None of the above
Explanation: u+x adds execute permission to user/owner, and go+r adds read permission to group and others.
