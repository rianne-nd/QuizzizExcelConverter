Question: Refer to the exhibit. A network administrator has configured ACL 9 as shown. Users on the 172.31.1.0 /24 network cannot forward traffic through router CiscoVille. What is the most likely cause of the traffic failure?

Option: The established keyword is not specified.
Option: The sequence of the ACEs is incorrect.*
Option: The port number for the traffic has not been identified with the eq keyword.
Option: The permit statement specifies an incorrect wildcard mask.
Explanation: Topic 4.1.3
When verifying an ACL, the statements are always listed in a sequential order. Even though there is an explicit permit for the traffic that is sourced from network 172.31.1.0 /24, it is being denied due to the previously implemented ACE of CiscoVille(config)# access-list 9 deny 172.31.0.0 0.0.255.255. Question: A network administrator is writing a standard ACL that will deny any traffic from the 172.16.0.0/16 network, but permit all other traffic. Which two commands should be used? (Choose two.)

Option: Router(config)# access-list 95 host 172.16.0.0

Option: Router(config)# access-list 95 172.16.0.0 255.255.255.255
Explanation: Topic 5.1.2
To deny traffic from the 172.16.0.0/16 network, the access-list 95 deny 172.16.0.0 0.0.255.255 command is used. To permit all other traffic, the access-list 95 permit any statement is added.
