Question: Which type of Internet-Based Connectivity provides high-speed, asymmetrical access, often used for home and small office WAN access?
Option: ISDN
Option: T3/E3 Leased Line
Option: Frame Relay
Option: Digital Subscriber Line (DSL) or Cable Broadband*
Explanation: DSL and Cable Broadband offer high-speed, asymmetrical throughput where download speeds are faster than upload speeds, making them ideal for home and small office WAN connections.

Question: You are tasked with setting up NAT in a network where internal clients access the internet and receive responses that must be routed back to the original requester. Which NAT configuration should you use?
Option: Dynamic NAT
Option: Static NAT
Option: PAT (Port Address Translation)*
Option: NAT64
Explanation: Port Address Translation (PAT) allows multiple internal clients to share a single public IP address by assigning unique source port numbers to track sessions and route returning traffic back to the original requester.

Question: A remote employee sets up a home office DSL connection connected via existing copper telephone lines to a provider Central Office. Option: Virtual Private LAN Service (VPLS)
Option: Point-to-Point Protocol over Ethernet (PPPoE)*
Option: High-Level Data Link Control (HDLC)
