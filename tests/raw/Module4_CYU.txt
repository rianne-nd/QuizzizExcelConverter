Question: What are the permit or deny statements in an ACL called?
Option: access control entries*
Option: arbitrary statements
Option: content control entries
Option: control statements
Explanation: The sequential list of permit and deny statements in an ACL are called access control entries (ACEs).

Question: Which ACL is capable of filtering based on TCP port number?
Option: extended ACL*
Option: standard ACL
Explanation: A standard ACL filters on source IPv4 addresses. An extended ACL filters on source and destination IPv4 address, protocol, and TCP/UDP ports.
